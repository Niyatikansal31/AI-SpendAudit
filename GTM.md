# GTM — Go To Market

## Exact Target User

Not "startups." The specific person is an **engineering manager or CTO at a 5-25 person B2B SaaS company** that raised a seed or Series A in the last 18 months. They are now accountable to a board for burn rate. They approved AI tool subscriptions in a rush during the past year — "everyone gets Cursor, everyone gets ChatGPT Team" — without a framework for evaluating whether the spend made sense. Now they are being asked by their CFO or investors to show they are managing costs without slowing engineering velocity. They are not a finance person but they are being asked to think like one.

Secondary user: **indie hackers and solo founders** running lean who are paying for multiple AI tools out of pocket and suspect they are over-subscribed but have not sat down to calculate it.

Submit "Show HN: I built a free AI spend auditor for engineering teams." Timing matters — Tuesday or Wednesday morning US time. The title should lead with the outcome, not the tech. A good HN thread drives 200-500 visitors in 24 hours. Target: 40 more users.

**Week 3 — Reddit:**
Post in r/ExperiencedDevs and r/startups with a genuine question framing: "We audited our AI tool spend and saved $400/mo — here's what we found (and a tool to check yours)." Authentic, not promotional. Target: 20 more users.

**Week 4 — Direct outreach:**
DM 20 CTOs/EMs on X who have tweeted about AI tool costs or Cursor/ChatGPT. Personalized message referencing their specific tweet. Not a mass blast — targeted and genuine. Target: 20 more users.
