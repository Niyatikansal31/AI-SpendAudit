# ECONOMICS

## What a Converted Lead is Worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise, sourced from companies that overforecast. Assume:

- Average credit package sold: **$3,000** (conservative — one quarter of enterprise AI spend for a 10-person team)
- Credex margin on resale: **20%** (discounted credits bought at 60-70% of face value, sold at 80%)
- Gross profit per transaction: **$600**
- Average customer buys **2 packages/year** (quarterly AI budget cycle)
- **LTV of one converted customer: $1,200**

This is conservative. A 25-person engineering team spending $5,000/month on AI tools is a $60,000/year relationship at face value. Even capturing 10% of that through credit resale is $6,000 ARR per customer.

## Customer Acquisition Cost by Channel

| Channel | Effort | Estimated CAC |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| Show HN (organic) | 1 post + responses | ~$0 direct, $50 time cost |
| Reddit posts | 2-3 posts/week | ~$0 direct, $30 time cost |
| Founder Slack DMs | 30 min/day | ~$0 direct, $40 time cost |
| X direct outreach | 1 hr/week | ~$0 direct, $25 time cost |
| Paid Twitter/X ads (later) | $500/month budget | ~$80-150 CAC |

At zero paid spend, CAC is effectively Credex team time. If one person spends 5 hours/week on distribution at $50/hr opportunity cost, monthly CAC budget is $1,000 — and at 5-10 conversions/month, CAC is $100-200 per converted customer against $1,200 LTV. That's a 6-12x return.

## Conversion Funnel

| Stage | Rate | Reasoning |
|-------|------|-----------|
| Landing → Audit completed | 40% | Low friction, no login, immediate value |
| Audit completed → Email captured | 25% | Email shown after value, not before |
| Email captured → Credex consultation booked | 15% | Only shown for >$500/mo savings cases |
| Consultation booked → Credit purchase | 30% | Already pre-qualified by savings data |

**Overall: audit completed → purchase = 1.1%**

At 1,000 audits/month: 11 purchases × $600 gross profit = **$6,600 gross profit/month**

At 5,000 audits/month: 55 purchases × $600 = **$33,000 gross profit/month**

## What Would Have to Be True for $1M ARR in 18 Months

$1M ARR = ~$83,000 revenue/month

At $600 gross profit per transaction and 2 transactions/customer/year:
- Need **~140 new customers/month** at steady state, or
- Need **~1,700 customers** over 18 months total

Working backwards from the funnel:
- 140 customers/month requires ~467 consultation bookings/month
- 467 bookings requires ~3,113 email captures/month
- 3,113 captures requires ~12,450 completed audits/month

**12,500 audits/month is achievable if:**
1. SpendScope gets featured in 2-3 "AI tools for startups" roundup articles (each drives 500-1,000 one-time visitors)
2. The shareable report URL creates a viral loop — each shared audit brings 3-5 new visitors
3. Credex builds a light affiliate/referral program where consultants and advisors share the tool with their portfolio companies
4. SEO compounds over 6 months on queries like "AI tool cost calculator" and "ChatGPT Team vs Plus"

The math is aggressive but not impossible for a tool with genuine value and a clear distribution channel through Credex's existing network.

## Summary

The unit economics work even at small scale. A free tool that costs ~$50/month to run (Render, MongoDB Atlas, Resend, Groq free tier) and converts 1% of audits into $600 gross profit needs only **8 purchases/month to break even on infrastructure**. Everything above that is margin. The constraint is distribution, not economics.
