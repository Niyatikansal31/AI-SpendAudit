## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
Yes. No credit card, no trial period, no login required. Enter your tools, get your audit instantly. We only ask for your email if you want a copy sent to you.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
Every number in the audit engine traces back to an official vendor pricing page, verified weekly. If something looks off, reach out and we'll fix it within 24 hours.

**Q: What do you do with my data?**
Your tool and spend data is used only to generate your audit. Shareable report URLs strip your email and company name — only tools and savings numbers are visible publicly. We don't sell your data.

**Q: Why is this free?**
SpendScope is built by Credex, which sells discounted AI credits to companies that are overspending. If your audit shows significant savings, we'll show you how Credex can help you save even more through discounted credits. If you're already spending well, we'll tell you that honestly.

**Q: Which AI tools does it cover?**
Currently: ChatGPT (Plus/Team/Enterprise/API), Cursor (Hobby/Pro/Business/Enterprise), GitHub Copilot (Individual/Business/Enterprise), Claude (Free/Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise/API), Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini (Pro/Ultra/API), and v0. More tools added regularly.
